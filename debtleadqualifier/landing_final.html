FAQ

Common questions.

What CRMs does this work with? +
Any CRM that exports CSV files — GoHighLevel, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, custom dialers, or spreadsheets. The tool auto-detects your column names and maps them. If auto-detect misses something, you fix it manually in the mapping UI and save it for next time.
Is my lead data safe? Who can see it? +
Nobody. The tool runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server, ever. When you close the tab, the data is gone. This isn't a marketing claim — it's how browser-only applications technically work. Your leads stay on your machine.
How does the scoring algorithm work? +
Every lead starts at a base score of 5. Points are added for high unsecured debt ($10k+, $20k+, $30k+), high DTI% (50%+, 80%+), lower credit scores (under 500, under 650), high credit utilization, and days of inactivity. Points are deducted for excessive call attempts. Final score is 1-10, tiered as Hot (9-10), Warm (7-8), Standard (5-6), or Cold (1-4).
What does "one-time payment" actually mean? +
Two plans. $9.88 one-time gives you a single session — one upload, one export, access expires after 24 hours and the link cannot be shared or reused. $49.88/month gives you unlimited sessions for as long as your subscription is active. Access is cut off automatically if billing lapses.
Can I use this for multiple companies or clients? +
Yes. The tool saves column mappings and export templates locally in your browser. You can save a separate mapping and export format for each client and switch between them in one click. There are no seat limits or usage restrictions.
